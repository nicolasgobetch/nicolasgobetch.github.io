Ergonomicon Backup

Questions and answers

Can I use Ergonomicon Backup?

No. It is a personal tool, used only by its owner with his own Google account. There is no sign-up and no download.

Why does it need access to Google Drive?

To store the encrypted off-site copy of Ergonomicon’s data in the owner’s own Google Drive, read it back for verification and restore, and delete expired copies.

Can it read my other files?

No. It uses the drive.file permission, which limits it to the files it created itself.

Can Google read the backups?

No. The files are encrypted on the owner’s server before upload; Google stores only encrypted data.

Is any data shared or sold?

No. Nothing is shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone, and nothing is used for advertising or for training models. See the privacy policy.

What does Ergonomicon contain?

Public job postings from company career pages, organised for the owner’s own research into the job market. No personal data of Google users is part of it.

How is access removed?

At myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the backup folder removes the stored data.
